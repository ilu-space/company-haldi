FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Straightforward answers to the things people actually want to know before trying somewhere new for lunch.

Is this a buffet?

No. Haldi is a counter-service restaurant with a curated daily menu — not a buffet. Each dish is made fresh that morning and served properly. We've found this is the only way to consistently serve food at the level we want.

How spicy is the food?

Every dish is clearly labelled with its spice level. We cook to authentic flavour profiles, not watered-down versions — but we always offer milder and hotter variants where possible, and staff can always advise.

Is there always something for vegetarians?

Always — at least half the daily menu is vegetarian or vegan, every day without exception. Indian cuisine has one of the world's richest plant-based traditions, and we take that seriously. Vegetarians will never feel like an afterthought here.

What are the opening hours?

We're focused on lunch — open Monday to Friday, 10:30am to 2:30pm. We're specifically designed for the Helsinki office lunch rush, so that window covers you completely. Hours will be confirmed closer to opening.

How much does it cost?

A full lunch — main dish, sides, bread or rice, and a drink — is priced between €11 and €14, in line with Helsinki's standard lounas pricing. Meal benefit cards are fully accepted, so most office workers will pay nothing out of pocket.

Are allergens listed?

Yes, all allergens are clearly listed for every dish, every day. Common allergens including gluten, dairy, and nuts are flagged prominently. If you have specific dietary needs, our staff are always happy to help you choose.

Can I pre-order to avoid queuing?

Yes — pre-ordering via our app will be available from day one. You'll be able to browse the day's menu, order ahead, and pick up with zero wait time. Details on the app will be shared with everyone on our notification list first.

Where exactly in Helsinki will Haldi be?

We're finalising our location and will announce it to our notification list before any public announcement. Sign up below to be among the first to find out — and to get early access to opening week offers.
